Frequently asked questions

Did you use "AI" to create this?

Yes. I understand if you have objections to that. You are entirely free to not use this app.

Do I need an account?

No. Every chart gets two secret links. The edit link is yours: anyone with it can change the chart, so keep it private. The view link is safe to share: people can look, print, and make their own copy.

I lost my edit link. Can I get it back?

This browser remembers the charts it has opened, so check the home page first. If it isn't there, there's no password to reset. Since there's no account and I don't want to store any information about you, there's not much I can do. Sorry!

What games does it work with?

Any game or edition. Character Flow doesn't know the rules, so everything on a chart is your own text.

How does printing work?

Each chart prints on a single letter sheet, landscape or portrait, with your character summary and notes. Pick the orientation next to the Print button.

Who are you and why did you "make" this?

I am Patrick Berry and I bulit this for my wife, who is really getting into D&D and these flow charts are a helpful way for her to learn how her characters work in combat. I have a background as a fairly mediocre developer, so using "AI" to "make" things is actually quite fun for me. Writing the code, for me, was always the least enjoyable part of solving a problem. If you've ever read Cryptonomicon from Neal Stephenson, I take after Lawrence Waterhouse...once I solve the problem in my head, the physical engineering is quite tedious.

For better or worse, Claude Code allows me to solve problems for myself or people close to me, that I otherwise would not have tackled. Again, I understand if you have issues with the use of "AI" and that's okay.
